Dubai isn't just skyscrapers and luxury malls — it's become one of the best cities for remote engineers who want reliable Wi-Fi, air conditioning, and a change of scenery without paying AED 2,000/month for a WeWork desk.

## Why free coworking works in Dubai

Mall lounges, hotel lobbies, and government-backed innovation hubs offer legit workspaces if you know where to look. The key is timing, dress code, and bringing your own power adapter.

## 5 spaces worth bookmarking

- Dubai Internet City — quiet corners with enterprise-grade Wi-Fi
- Mall business lounges — best for quick calls between errands
- Creative district cafés — ideal for deep work before noon
- Library study zones — underrated for focus sessions
- Hotel lobbies — premium feel, just buy a coffee

## Practical tips

Pack noise-canceling headphones, a mobile hotspot backup, and a lightweight blazer if you're hopping between client video calls and public spaces. Fridays are quieter; Sundays feel like Mondays.
